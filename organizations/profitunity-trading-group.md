# Profitunity Trading Group

**[Bill Williams](/people/bill-williams.md)**'s trading education business at the time of the talk. Offering described in the talk:[^practical-fractal-video]

- A 3-month home-study package: the book *[Trading Chaos](/books/trading-chaos.md)*, a 300-page manual, a "chaos workbook" with 90 days of daily instructions, 12 video tapes, 8 audio tapes, and 3 computer disks.
- Followed by an in-person tutorial (staffed by 6) where attendees trade their own live account with ongoing support.
- ~600 people had completed the tutorial by the time of the talk, most going on to trade professionally or privately.
- A follow-up book applying the method to equities was in progress.
