# The Practical Fractal: The Holy Grail to Trading

A recorded talk by **[Bill Williams](/people/bill-williams.md)**, PhD, of the **[Profitunity Trading Group](/organizations/profitunity-trading-group.md)**, given a few years after the first edition of his book **[Trading Chaos](/books/trading-chaos.md)**. Raw source record: [`raw/sources/practical-fractal-bill-williams.md`](../../raw/sources/practical-fractal-bill-williams.md).

## What the talk covers

Williams argues that markets are not random or predictable but *fractal* — a natural phenomenon governed by chaos theory, the same mathematics that describes rivers and coastlines. From that premise he builds a trading method around five "dimensions" of market structure rather than price alone, and a trading psychology built on not fighting or predicting the market.
