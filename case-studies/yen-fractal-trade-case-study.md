# Japanese yen fractal trade

Live-charted example from the talk, on a daily Japanese yen chart from the year before the talk, tracking a position built entry-by-entry on successive [fractal](/concepts/fractal.md) breakouts, one contract at a time:[^practical-fractal-video]

- **End of January:** ~$513 open loss
- **End of February:** ~$6,738 open profit (4 long positions)
- **End of March:** ~$70,000 open profit (5 long positions), after a strongly trending month
- **Exit, April 19:** ~$121,000 total profit across 6 long positions, closing out a roughly 3.5-month trade

Williams uses this as evidence the method "works on every commodity and every time frame," and pairs it with two spread case studies (10-month Swiss franc/yen and Deutsche mark/yen spreads, using price-difference charts with no OHLC/volume but the same two-fractal signal logic) to argue the same for currency spreads.[^practical-fractal-video]

Note: these are the speaker's own claimed results as presented in the talk, not independently verified figures.

[^practical-fractal-video]: Bill Williams, *The Practical Fractal: The Holy Grail to Trading*.
