# San Juan Investments testimonial

Charles Parker and Steve Winland, tutorial graduates based in Houston, TX, started an offshore fund called "San Juan Investments" about four years after taking Williams' tutorial. Williams says the fund ranked #1 out of 200+ funds tracked by *Futures Magazine* in one October, was still #1 (and #5 the following October) a year later, and was among 1994's top performers.[^practical-fractal-video]

Cited alongside the [Janet](/case-studies/janet-eurodollar-spread-case-study.md) anecdote as evidence for the **[Profitunity Trading Group](/organizations/profitunity-trading-group.md)** tutorial's track record.
