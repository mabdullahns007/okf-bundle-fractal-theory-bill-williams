# Janet's Eurodollar spread trade

Anecdote from the talk about "Janet," a former general contractor from Little Rock, Arkansas, trading a self-funded account (her husband declined to contribute family money) with only about $4,600. Given a [five magic bullets](/concepts/five-magic-bullets.md) bottom signal in Eurodollar spreads, she bought 3 spreads at −68, risking roughly $75 per contract (~$225 total plus commissions).[^practical-fractal-video]

After the tutorial, she reportedly recognized she had more margin capacity than she'd used, sized up the position on her own, and later closed most of it at −27 — netting about $16,250 after commissions, growing the account from $4,621 to roughly $20,800 in about 3–4 months.[^practical-fractal-video]

Williams tells this story as a psychology example as much as a technical one — see [Trading psychology](/concepts/trading-psychology-holy-grail.md).

Note: as with the [yen case study](/case-studies/yen-fractal-trade-case-study.md), these are the speaker's own claimed figures, not independently verified.
