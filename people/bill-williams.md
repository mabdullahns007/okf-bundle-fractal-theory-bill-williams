# Bill Williams

Trader and PhD who founded the **[Profitunity Trading Group](/organizations/profitunity-trading-group.md)** and wrote **[Trading Chaos](/books/trading-chaos.md)**. His method applies chaos theory and fractal geometry to markets, built around the idea that price is the last of several "dimensions" to change, not the first.[^practical-fractal-video]

## Trading track record claims (from the talk)

- Says he personally hasn't been "aggravated at the market" in 7+ years, and that only one limit-move went against his positions in that period.[^practical-fractal-video]
- Describes his win rate as currently under 50% — deliberately lower than the ~68–69% he had years earlier when he made less money — framing this as evidence that win rate is the wrong thing to optimize.[^practical-fractal-video]
- By the time of the talk, roughly 600 people had gone through his private tutorial.[^practical-fractal-video]

## Style

Prefers a calm, distraction-free trading environment (classical music, no financial news/TV) and describes his job as removing personal *reaction* (fear, hesitation, hope) from trade execution rather than predicting direction. See [Trading psychology: "want what the market wants"](/concepts/trading-psychology-holy-grail.md).
