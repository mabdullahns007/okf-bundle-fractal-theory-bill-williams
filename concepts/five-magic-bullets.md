# Five magic bullets

When all [five dimensions of market structure](/concepts/five-dimensions-of-market-structure.md) reverse direction at the same time, Williams calls it the "five magic bullets" and says that, in his experience, this reliably — without exception — kills a trend.[^practical-fractal-video]

This is the setup behind the [Janet Eurodollar spread case study](/case-studies/janet-eurodollar-spread-case-study.md), where a bottom signal of this kind is described being used for a real trade.

[^practical-fractal-video]: Bill Williams, *The Practical Fractal: The Holy Grail to Trading*.
