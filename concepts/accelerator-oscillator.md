# Accelerator oscillator

A 5-bar moving average of the [momentum oscillator (5/34)](/concepts/momentum-oscillator.md) — effectively a second derivative of price. Williams calls it "tomorrow's Wall Street Journal": because acceleration changes before momentum, which changes before price, it's the earliest-warning of his [five dimensions of market structure](/concepts/five-dimensions-of-market-structure.md).[^practical-fractal-video]

Together with the momentum oscillator it reconstructs a standard MACD — see that page for the simple-vs-exponential moving-average distinction Williams insists on.
