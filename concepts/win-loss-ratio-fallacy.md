# Win/loss ratio fallacy

Williams argues win rate is a misleading, even vanity, metric — separate from whether a strategy is actually profitable. He illustrates this with a writer at the *Commodity Traders Consumer Report* who deliberately locked in small wins and let losses run specifically to win that year's "best win/loss ratio" award (67% wins) — and still finished the year with a loss.[^practical-fractal-video]

He contrasts this with his own numbers: a win rate he estimates at under 50% at the time of the talk, versus roughly 68–69% years earlier when he made substantially less money — the lesson he draws is to focus on cutting losses fast and letting the [five dimensions of market structure](/concepts/five-dimensions-of-market-structure.md) run, not on being "right" often.[^practical-fractal-video]
