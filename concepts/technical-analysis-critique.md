# Technical analysis critique

Williams claims roughly 90% of technical analysts lose money, and argues the underlying flaw is that classical technical analysis fits a template of *past* price behavior onto the future — while, in his framing, "the future will not be like the past." He compares this to scripting every play of a football game before it starts.[^practical-fractal-video]

This critique is the stated motivation for trading the [five dimensions of market structure](/concepts/five-dimensions-of-market-structure.md) (which react to *changing* market state) rather than pattern-matching against historical price shapes — and for rejecting overbought/oversold readings; see [Market definition (Fred & Barney)](/concepts/market-definition.md).

[^practical-fractal-video]: Bill Williams, *The Practical Fractal: The Holy Grail to Trading*.
