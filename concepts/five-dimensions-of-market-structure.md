# Five dimensions of market structure

Williams' central framework: trade five things instead of price alone, in the order he claims they actually change (see [Chaos theory — three principles](/concepts/chaos-theory-three-principles.md) for the causal argument). He frames this, with a nod to relativity, as trading "mass, energy, space, and time" rather than a single number.[^practical-fractal-video]

1. **[Fractal](/concepts/fractal.md)** — the "space" dimension (initiating, breakout, and responsive fractals)
2. **[Balance line](/concepts/balance-line.md)** — the "strange attractor" dimension
3. **[Momentum oscillator (5/34)](/concepts/momentum-oscillator.md)**
4. **Direction of momentum change**
5. **[Accelerator oscillator](/concepts/accelerator-oscillator.md)**

When all five reverse direction simultaneously, Williams calls it the **[five magic bullets](/concepts/five-magic-bullets.md)** — in his account, a signal that reliably kills a trend.
